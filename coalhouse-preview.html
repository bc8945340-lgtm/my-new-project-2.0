Sourcing

We buy the harvest,
not the invoice.

Every lot on our shelf is traceable to a farm or washing station. We pay above the Fair Trade floor, hold long-term contracts with three cooperatives, and rotate one guest origin each season so the pour-over bar never goes stale.

Roasting happens on-site, five mornings a week, on a 1957 drum rebuilt by our head roaster. Nothing sits in the hopper longer than three weeks before it's cracked open.
